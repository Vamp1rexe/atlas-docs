# Atlas

**Atlas is the in-house anticheat and staff system of DustSMP.**
It keeps the server fair: it detects cheats and unfair mods, protects the economy from dupes and gives the staff team the tools to handle reports.

When you see `Atlas` in chat, on a kick screen or in the rules, this system is acting.

---

## What Atlas does

| Area | What it means for you |
|------|----------------------|
| **Anticheat** | Movement, combat and packet checks run for every player. Cheats like fly, speed, reach, kill aura or auto clickers get flagged. |
| **Mod check** | Your client and its mods are checked on join. Hacked clients and unfair mods are not allowed. |
| **X-ray detection** | Unusual ore mining is reported to the staff team. |
| **Anti-dupe** | Duplicated items are detected and removed. |
| **Exploit protection** | Invalid or crash packets are blocked before they reach the server. |
| **Staff tools** | Staff can freeze players, check inventories and review reports and history. |

Atlas does not read your chat or private files. It only looks at what your client sends to the server while you play.

---

## Allowed and not allowed

**Allowed:** performance and visual mods that give no gameplay advantage, for example Sodium, Iris, shaders, minimaps without entity or cave radar, Lunar and Badlion without cheat features.

**Not allowed:**
- hacked clients (Meteor, Wurst, LiquidBounce and similar)
- x-ray, ESP, tracers, kill aura, auto totem, auto clicker
- Baritone and other bots
- mods that hide other mods from the server

Not sure about a mod? Ask in the Discord **before** you join with it.

---

## What happens when Atlas flags you

1. **Kick** with the message `[Atlas] Unfair advantage detected` or a mod notice.
2. **Repeated or clear cases** lead to a temporary or permanent ban.
3. **Hacked clients** are banned directly.

Every punishment is logged with a reason and reviewed by the staff team.

---

## You think it was a mistake?

No anticheat is perfect. If you were kicked or banned and did not cheat:

1. Join the Discord: **[discord.gg/dXRU5nczV7](https://discord.gg/dXRU5nczV7)**
2. Open a ticket and add your Minecraft name, the time and the message you saw.
3. Staff checks the logs and lifts the punishment if it was a false flag.

Do not create alt accounts to get around a ban. This leads to a permanent ban on all accounts.

---

## Report a cheater

Ingame: `/report <player>` and pick a reason.
Every report goes to the staff team.
